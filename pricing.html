Frequently Answered Questions

Confidentiality, Travel & Custody

Key information regarding our commissioning process.

How do the recording sessions feel?

Clients frequently share that the experience feels like an unhurried conversation by the fireside with a trusted friend. Our interviewers are trained historians who listen with genuine warmth and patience, never rushing or interrogating.

Where do the sessions take place?

We travel worldwide to conduct sessions in the privacy of your home, family estate, or retreat. We bring mobile soundproofing and acoustic equipment that transforms any quiet room into a broadcast sanctuary without disruption.

Does Okay Legacy keep any copy of the vault?

By default, unless you explicitly choose our encrypted cloud backup service, Okay Legacy operates under a strict zero-retention policy. Once your physical vault and masters are delivered, all working files on our encrypted drives are cryptographically wiped.

How does the investment schedule work?

Commissions are structured across three production milestones: 40% upon commission agreement, 30% upon completion of recording sessions, and 30% upon final physical vault sealing and handoff.
